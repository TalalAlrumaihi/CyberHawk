How CyberHawk Works

01
🚁
Deploy the Drone

The UAV is launched and the Raspberry Pi boots into Kali Linux. The wireless adapter enters monitor mode, ready to passively capture all 802.11 frames in the area — no association required.

02
📡
Scan Networks

As the drone flies, our custom scanning engine sweeps all channels across both 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands, logging SSID, BSSID, encryption type, WPS status, and signal strength for every detected access point.

03
🔍
Detect Vulnerabilities

CyberHawk's intelligence engine classifies each network: WEP = High Risk, WPS-enabled = Medium Risk, WPA3 = Low Risk. Weak targets undergo fully automated controlled penetration testing.

04
📋
Generate Report

Within seconds of landing, a full security audit report is generated — with risk classifications, vulnerability details, and specific remediation steps — instantly displayed on-screen.

// Features

Platform Capabilities

Offensive
Penetration Testing Engine
  • Detects and cracks WEP networks — a protocol the industry abandoned, yet still widely deployed
  • Bypasses WPS PIN vulnerabilities in as few as 11,000 attempts — outpacing conventional scanners
  • Identifies rogue and misconfigured access points that ground-based tools miss entirely
  • Full credential recovery in ~3 minutes — twice as fast as leading alternatives
Defensive
Threat Analysis & Reporting
  • Precision risk classification engine: High / Medium / Low
  • Zero false positives — every alert is a verified, real-world threat
  • Instant on-screen security report generated the moment a scan completes
  • Plain-language remediation steps built for non-technical decision makers
AI-Powered
Intelligent Chatbot Assistant
  • Answers wireless security questions instantly with contextual intelligence
  • Guides users through vulnerability findings and remediation steps
  • Translates complex technical results into clear, actionable language
UAV Mobility
Unmatched Aerial Coverage
  • Scans 1,200 m² per flight — over 167% more than any ground-based alternative
  • Clears physical barriers: walls, floors, and signal blockers are no obstacle
  • Simultaneous dual-band sweep across 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz — nothing escapes
  • Flight path dynamically optimized for maximum access point discovery
  • All processing handled onboard — no external server, no latency
